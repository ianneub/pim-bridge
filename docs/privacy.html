Privacy Policy

PIM Bridge · last updated October 16, 2026

PIM Bridge is a Mac app that runs entirely on your own computer. There is no PIM Bridge server. The app talks to Google to reach your Gmail and to nobody else, and it hands your data only to programs on your Mac that you have connected to it.

What it reads from Google

When you sign in with Google, PIM Bridge asks for the gmail.modify scope. With it, PIM Bridge can:

  • search your mail, and read messages and threads: headers, bodies, labels and the names of attachments;
  • list and read your drafts, create new drafts, and change existing ones;
  • move messages to Trash and back out of Trash.

Google's gmail.modify scope would also allow sending mail. PIM Bridge has no feature that sends mail, deletes a draft, or permanently deletes anything, so it never does these things.

What it does with it

PIM Bridge passes what it reads to the programs on your Mac that you've connected to it: its command-line tool, a menu-bar app, or AI agents such as Claude Code. It only does this when one of those programs asks, and nothing outside your Mac can connect to it.

When you connect an AI agent, mail the agent reads through PIM Bridge is processed by that agent and its provider (for Claude Code, Anthropic) under the agent's own terms and privacy policy. You choose which agents to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time.

PIM Bridge doesn't sell your data, share it with anyone, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

What it stores

Everything PIM Bridge keeps stays on your Mac:

  • the sign-in token Google issues, in your Mac's login Keychain, readable only by PIM Bridge;
  • the list of accounts you've signed in (a short name and the email address);
  • a log of every move to or from Trash (time, account, message id, sender and subject), deleted automatically after 90 days, so you can see and undo what was moved;
  • message headers, held in memory for up to five minutes to avoid fetching them again, and never written to disk.

Its activity log records events such as sign-ins and draft ids, not the content of your mail.

Reminders, Calendar and Contacts

With your permission in macOS, PIM Bridge also reads and changes your Apple reminders, calendars and contacts on the Mac. That data is handled the same way: it goes only to the programs on your Mac that you've connected, and PIM Bridge keeps only a small file matching calendar event ids to events.

Removing access

Sign out with gmail sign-out, which also revokes the token at Google, or remove PIM Bridge at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either ends the app's access to your Gmail. Revoking the Reminders, Calendars or Contacts permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security ends its access to those.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PIM Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
