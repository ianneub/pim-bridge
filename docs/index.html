PIM Bridge

Apple Reminders, Calendar, Contacts and Gmail for AI agents and scripts on your own Mac.

PIM Bridge is a small, open-source Mac app. It runs in the background and lets programs on the same Mac, such as Claude Code, other AI agents, the command line and menu-bar apps, read and update your reminders, calendars, contacts and Gmail. It runs entirely on your Mac: there is no PIM Bridge server or account, and it accepts connections only from the Mac it runs on.

Source, install steps and the security design: github.com/ianneub/pim-bridge.

Gmail

PIM Bridge can sign in to your Google account so the programs you connect to it can search and read your mail, write and edit drafts, and move messages to and from Trash. It asks Google for the gmail.modify scope. It has no way to send mail: drafts wait for you to review and send them yourself in Gmail. It can't delete messages or drafts permanently. Moving mail to Trash from anything other than its menu-bar companion needs your fingerprint on the Mac's Touch ID sensor.
